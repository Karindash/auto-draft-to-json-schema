### 2. Development Scheme
As the key process in this system is available at Business Process Definition, there must be development path through this generally business development. Here are phases in multiple MVP:
    a) MVP 0: Key Feature is well defined, project has been envisioned into proper business proposal (including further details of what should be visible or not)
    b) MVP 1: Key Feature works well, properly, projected as the implementation of the entire (temporary) vision before stakeholder. First test to the market as it includes at least:
        - 1 Additional feature
        - 1 Adaptive feature
    c) MVP 2: Shareholders as the opinion between founder mixed; vision hardened in key product, side features development begins as the key and R&D going paralelly.
    d) MVP n+2: Decided due ton shareholders meeting & agreement

Disclaimer, development cycles fulfillment would be assisted by AI. As long as it hasn't been solved humanly or technically, we would rely on AI problem solving agents.
